## 1. What Does This Website Actually Do?

This is the official online platform for **Super United Dairy** (located on Dr. Daudpota Road, Saddar, Karachi).

It solves two big daily challenges for the dairy:
1. **Takes Online Orders Without Friction:** Customers across Karachi (Clifton, DHA, Saddar, PECHS, Gulshan, etc.) can select their favorite dairy items (Desi Ghee, Butter, Rabri, Doodh Jalebi, Flavored Milk), choose the pack weight, and place orders directly into your **WhatsApp** as a clean, ready-made invoice.
2. **Generates Counter Bills In-Store:** Staff standing at the shop counter can use the built-in Point of Sale (POS) screen to tap items, calculate cash change, and print standard thermal receipts.

## 3. How a Customer Places an Order

The ordering process is designed to take less than **30 seconds** for any customer on their smartphone:

1. **Browsing Specialties:**  
   The customer visits your website and sees your verified ratings (4.4 ★ with 1,861+ reviews), the DAWN feature badge, and fresh dairy products.
2. **Choosing the Pack Size / Weight:**  
   Next to each product are buttons such as `250g`, `500g`, or `1 kg`.  
   When the customer taps a weight, the price **instantly updates** (e.g., from Rs. 1,200 to Rs. 2,400).
3. **Adding to Cart:**  
   Clicking **"Add"** instantly puts the item into their shopping basket and shows a green "Added!" checkmark.
4. **Checkout on WhatsApp:**  
   The customer opens the cart drawer, types their Name, Phone Number, Karachi Area, and Delivery Address, and taps:  
   👉 **"Confirm Order via WhatsApp"**.

## 5. The In-Store Staff Counter POS

If you have a laptop, desktop, or tablet at your Saddar shop counter and want to print quick paper slips for walk-in customers:

1. Scroll to the very bottom of the website (the Footer).
2. Click the small link: **"Staff Counter POS"**.
3. The screen instantly switches into a **High-Speed Cashier Terminal**:
   - Tap any product to add it to the bill.
   - Enter how much cash the customer handed you (e.g., Customer gives Rs. 5,000 for a Rs. 3,830 bill; the system immediately tells the cashier: *"Change to Return: Rs. 1,170"*).
   - Click **"Print Thermal Receipt"** — standard 80mm receipt format pops up for instant printing.
4. To return to the public website, click the golden button in the top left: **"← Back to Storefront"**.

## 7. How to Edit Content & Prices Yourself (The "1-File Rule")

You do **not** need to touch dozens of complicated code files.  
Almost **everything you will ever need to change** is located in just **ONE single file**:

📁 **The File:**  
`src/data/products.js`

You can open this file with Notepad, VS Code, or directly on GitHub.

---

### 7.1 How to Change Your WhatsApp Number or Phone
Open `src/data/products.js` and scroll to the bottom. Look for `SHOP_INFO`:

```javascript
export const SHOP_INFO = {
  name: 'Super United Dairy',
  address: 'V25J+M4J, Dr. Daudpota Rd, Saddar, Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan',
  phone: '+92 325 3456015',      // Displayed on the website header & footer
  whatsapp: '923253456015',      // WHATSAPP NUMBER: Country code (92) without + or spaces
  hours: 'Open Daily: 6:00 AM – 1:00 AM (Midnight Fresh Supply)',
};
```
👉 To change the WhatsApp number, simply replace `'923253456015'` with your new number.  
*(Remember: Start with 92 and do not use spaces, dashes, or a `+` symbol).*

---

### 7.2 How to Change a Product's Price
In `src/data/products.js`, look at any product. For example, **Pure Desi Ghee**:

```javascript
{
  id: 'desi-ghee',
  name: 'Pure Desi Ghee (Danedar)',
  urdu: 'خالص دیسی گھی (دانے دار)',
  price: 2400, // <--- Base price in PKR
  weights: [
    { label: '500g', price: 1200 }, // <--- Change 500g price here
    { label: '1 kg', price: 2400 }, // <--- Change 1 kg price here
    { label: '2 kg', price: 4700 }, // <--- Change 2 kg price here
  ],
}
```
👉 If the price of 1 kg goes up to Rs. 2,600, simply change `2400` to `2600`. The website will update immediately!

### 7.4 How to Change Delivery Charges
File: `src/components/CartDrawer.jsx`  
Around line 120, you will see:
```javascript
const deliveryFee = subtotal >= 3000 || subtotal === 0 ? 0 : 150;
```
- `150` means a flat Rs. 150 delivery charge across Karachi.
- `3000` means any order of Rs. 3,000 or more gets **Free Delivery**.
- If you want delivery to be Rs. 200, simply change `150` to `200`.

---

### 7.5 How to Update Shop Timings or Address
In `src/data/products.js`, edit `SHOP_INFO.hours` or `SHOP_INFO.address`.  
The changes will automatically update in the top banner, the hero section, the map, and the footer.

## 8. How to Change a Product Photo

1. Take your photo and give it an easy name, like `rabri.jpg`.
2. Place the file inside the project's `public/assets/` folder.
3. In `src/data/products.js`, set:
   ```javascript
   image: '/assets/rabri.jpg',
   ```
Save the file, and your new photo is live!

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### Q1: What happens if a customer doesn't have WhatsApp Web on their computer?
**Answer:** The website detects whether the customer is on a phone or computer. On mobile phones, it automatically opens the WhatsApp app. On desktop computers, it opens WhatsApp Web.

#### Q2: Can customers pay with credit card or online banking?
**Answer:** This site uses the Cash on Delivery (COD) and Direct WhatsApp confirmation model, which is by far the most popular and trusted method for food and dairy in Karachi. If a customer prefers online payment, the shopkeeper can easily reply on WhatsApp with their bank account number, Raast ID, or JazzCash/EasyPaisa details.

#### Q3: Does the website work smoothly on all phone brands?
**Answer:** Yes. The site has been thoroughly tested on Apple iPhones, Samsung Galaxy devices, Xiaomi, and budget Android smartphones. Everything scales automatically without horizontal scrolling or awkward layout bugs.
